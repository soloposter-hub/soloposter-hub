SoloPoster Hub

A personal tool for one creator.

What it does

SoloPoster Hub is a personal productivity tool that helps a single creator upload and schedule their own videos to their own TikTok account from a computer.

How it works

The creator signs in with TikTok and gives permission. The tool then uploads the videos and captions the creator chooses, using TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. Access can be removed at any time in TikTok's settings.
