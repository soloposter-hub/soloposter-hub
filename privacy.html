Privacy Policy

SoloPoster Hub · Last updated: October 11, 2026

1. About this app

SoloPoster Hub is a personal tool used by a single creator to send their own videos to their own TikTok account as drafts.

2. What information is used

When the account owner connects their TikTok account, TikTok provides an access token that lets the app post on their behalf. The app also handles the video files that the account owner chooses to send.

3. How information is stored

The access token is stored only on the account owner's own computer. The app does not run a public server or database, and does not collect personal information from any other people.

4. How information is used

Information is used only to send the account owner's videos to their TikTok inbox as drafts and to check the status of those uploads. It is not used for advertising or analytics.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties. Videos are sent only to TikTok through its official API.

6. Your choices

The account owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok's settings under the connected apps and services section, and can delete the stored token by deleting the local token file.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
